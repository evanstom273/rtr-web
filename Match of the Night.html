Match of the Night

Win the match and steal the show. Every bout gets rated out of five by a crowd that has seen everything and is not easily impressed.

How it plays
  • Every move runs a timing bar. Stop it in the gold to land your move. When they attack, stop it in the gold to reverse.
  • Moves need the right positions. Corner moves need them in the corner, dives need you up top or them on the floor. Setup gets you there.
  • Landed moves build momentum. At full momentum your signature unlocks, and landing it banks a finisher (up to three). Counter their finisher and you steal one.
  • Pinned? Hold the button and let go inside the gold before three. Stuck in a hold? Mash.
  • You're rated on the match, not just the win: a hot crowd, a heat segment and a comeback, limb work that pays off, near-falls, and a finish that feels earned. Spam the same move and the crowd will tell you.
